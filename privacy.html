Oi privacy policy

Last updated 27 October 2026

Oi does not collect any data about you.

What stays on your phone

Everything you set up in Oi (your jobs, bin colours, dates, names and settings) is stored on your iPhone and nowhere else. There are no accounts, no servers and no cloud sync. Oi does not track you, and it contains no analytics, advertising or third-party SDKs.

Reminders are local notifications created and delivered by your iPhone. They are never sent through our servers, because we do not have any.

Purchases

Oi Pro is a one-off in-app purchase. Payments, receipts and refunds are handled entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details. See Apple's privacy policy for how Apple handles purchase data.

The home screen widget

The widget reads your jobs from storage on your phone, shared only between the Oi app and its widget. Nothing leaves the device.

Deleting your data

Delete the app and all of its data goes with it.

Children

Oi does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect information at all.

Changes

If this ever changes, we will update this page and the date above before the change ships.

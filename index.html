Quick answers

I'm not getting reminders. Open the iPhone Settings app, tap Oi, then Notifications, and turn on Allow Notifications.

I've got a new phone. Download Oi again from the App Store with the same Apple ID; you won't pay twice. If you set the new phone up from an iPhone backup, your reminders come back with it; otherwise set them up again (it takes a minute).

My bin day changed. Tap the bins on the Coming up screen, change the day and tap Save.
